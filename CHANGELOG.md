# Changelog

All notable changes to Reelmint are documented in this file.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the
project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0]

First public release.

### Recording
- Screen recording up to 1440p and 120 fps, with device audio, microphone or both
- Pause and resume, countdown and orientation lock
- Facecam overlay, draw-on-screen annotations and floating controls
- Automatic stop after a time limit, on shake, or when the screen locks
- Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts
- "Recording saved" notification with a preview and Share and Delete actions

### Library and editor
- Recordings saved to Movies › Reelmint and kept in sync with the Gallery
- Library grouped by date, with search, sort, favourites, multi-select and a storage overview
- Player with variable playback speed and file details
- Trim, rotate, remove or extract audio, and save a frame as a photo
- Compress, change speed and merge clips, with progress and cancel

### Experience
- App lock with biometrics or the device screen lock
- Six accent themes, dark and light appearance, and haptic feedback
- Runs at the display's highest refresh rate
